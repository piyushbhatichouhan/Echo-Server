# Privacy & Data Collection Policy for EchoServer

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

At **EchoServer** ("we", "us", or "our"), we believe in privacy by design, minimal data collection, and radical transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines precisely:
1. **What data we collect**
2. **Why we collect it**
3. **What we use it for**
4. **How it is protected and retained**
5. **How you can delete your data**

---

## 1. Summary of Our Privacy Philosophy
- **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** We do not sell your personal data, run advertising networks, or share user details with data brokers.
- **Privacy-Preserving Telemetry:** Public visitor traffic to your deployed apps is tracked using irreversible, one-way SHA-256 IP hashes. Raw IP addresses of your app visitors are **never** stored in analytics.
- **Your Code is Private:** Your application files, environment secrets, and databases belong entirely to you.
- **Immediate Deletion:** When you delete a project or account, the associated data is permanently and immediately destroyed.

## 5. Passwords & Cryptographic Security
1. **Passwords:** Your password is never stored or transmitted in plaintext. It is salted and hashed using standard industry cryptography (`bcrypt` / `pgcrypto`). Platform administrators and operators cannot view or recover your raw password.
2. **Session Security:** Authenticated sessions use signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with time-bound expiration.
3. **Transport Encryption:** All traffic to and from the EchoServer web console and deployed endpoints is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.

---

## 6. How We Share Your Information
We **do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade** your personal data or user code to any third party. Data is only handled in the following operational scenarios:
- **DNS / SSL Certificate Authorities:** Automated SSL/TLS certificates require querying Let's Encrypt / ACME protocols with the requested domain hostname.
- **Legal Compliance:** In the rare event of a legally binding subpoena, court order, or formal law enforcement request concerning unlawful activity (e.g., hosting malware, CSAM, or cyberattacks), data may be disclosed as required by applicable law.

---

## 7. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion Policy
- **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account from the user settings at any time.
- **Immediate Permanent Purge:** When you delete a project, file, or your entire account:
  - All source code, uploaded files, and cloud storage objects are **immediately erased** from the filesystem.
  - All environment variables and database rows associated with the project/user are **immediately deleted**.
  - All assigned routing rules and custom domain associations are removed from the reverse proxy.
  - Deployment logs and project analytics are purged immediately.
- **Disaster Recovery Backups:** Encrypted system backups taken for disaster recovery are cycled on automated rolling schedules and overwritten permanently.

---

## 8. Your Rights Regarding Your Data
As a user of EchoServer, you have the right to:
1. **Access:** View all data, projects, files, and environment variables stored under your account at any time via your dashboard.
2. **Rectification:** Update your username, email address, password, or configuration directly in Settings.
3. **Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** Delete any project, asset, or your entire account with immediate effect.
4. **Data Portability:** Download or clone your code and cloud assets at any time.
